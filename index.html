How it works

Race the
Interstate

Build a deck from states along your chosen route. Flip cards simultaneously. Higher MPH clears each stop. First player past the final stop wins.

01
Choose Your Route
Pick an interstate — I-90, I-95, I-80, I-10, I-40, or I-70. Each route has a unique set of states and escalating thresholds from start to finish.
02
Declare Your Weapon
Choose a weapon type — Cruise, Turbo, Nitro, Ghost, and more. Your entire deck is built from that one weapon across all route states.
03
Flip & Race
Both players flip simultaneously. Beat the stop threshold to advance. When you're at the same stop, it's head-to-head — higher MPH wins.
04
Sudden Death
Tied on MPH in a head-to-head? Both players flip again immediately. Highest MPH takes the stop. Repeat until resolved.
05
Wildcard States
Alaska, Hawaii, and North Dakota are universal wildcards — playable at any stop on any route. Essential for clearing the toughest final stops.
06
Win the Race
First player to advance past the final stop wins. Your deck recycles automatically — the race never ends from running out of cards.
